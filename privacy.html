Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2025

At CKNS Realty & Deco Planning ("CKNS", "we", "us", or "our"), your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit cknsworld.store, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways to provide and improve our services:

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address when you place an order or reach out via our contact form or live chat.
  • Order information — the products you purchase, selected colour variants, and order history.
  • Communications — messages you send us through Tawk.to live chat, email, or the contact form.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and time spent on the site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files that help us remember your cart, preferences, and improve site performance.

Payment information

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number, CVC, or bank details. Stripe's own privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled.

Fraud protection: Payments flagged by our fraud protection systems are manually reviewed for your safety before an order ships.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders, including shipping and customer support.
  • Communicate with you about your order, respond to inquiries, and provide live chat support.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorised transactions, and other security risks.
  • Improve our website, product catalogue, and overall shopping experience.
  • Send occasional updates or promotions — only if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted service providers who help us operate the business, including:

  • Stripe — for secure payment processing.
  • Tawk.to — for live chat customer support.
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your orders.
  • Analytics providers — to help us understand site usage (in aggregate, anonymised form where possible).

These providers are only given the information necessary to perform their services and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features (like the shopping cart) may not function correctly.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our site uses HTTPS encryption, and payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
